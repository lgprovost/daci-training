About DACI

Safer People. Stronger Teams.

Professional, practical training led by Brad Leakey, Founder & Lead Instructor.

Our mission
“To provide professional, practical de-escalation and crisis intervention training that enhances safety, reduces risk, strengthens communication, and improves outcomes for the people and organizations we serve.”
Founder & Lead Instructor

Brad Leakey

Brad brings decades of frontline emergency services and crisis response experience to DACI’s approach to organizational safety training.

His focus is practical: help people recognize early warning signs, communicate with confidence, and prepare for a coordinated response that reflects their role and environment.

Experience with purpose

  • Certified Crisis Prevention Institute (CPI) Instructor
  • Decades of Frontline Emergency Services & Crisis Response Experience
  • Partner Organization: CPR Plus+ (azcprplus.com)

DACI is a sister organization to CPR Plus+. We serve businesses and organizations across Phoenix Metro and Arizona with live onsite group training.

12 Reasons Organizations Choose DACI

01

Prevention Before Crisis

We teach participants how to identify warning signs and intervene early, reducing the likelihood of escalation and improving outcomes.

02

Real-World Experience

Our curriculum is built upon decades of frontline experience in emergency services, healthcare, behavioral health, education, and crisis response.

03

Proven De-Escalation Techniques

Participants learn practical communication skills that help calm tense situations while maintaining dignity and respect.

04

Safety-Focused Training

DACI emphasizes personal safety, situational awareness, and team-based responses that help reduce injuries and liability.

05

Trauma-Informed Approach

Understanding how trauma influences behavior allows staff to respond more effectively and compassionately.

06

Easy-to-Learn, Easy-to-Use Skills

Our techniques are simple, realistic, and designed for immediate application in real-world situations.

07

Adaptable Across Industries

DACI principles can be customized for healthcare, schools, behavioral health, public safety, corporate environments, and community organizations.

08

Respect and Professionalism

We teach interventions that preserve dignity while maintaining a safe environment for everyone involved.

09

Interactive, Hands-On Learning

Participants gain confidence through realistic scenarios, skill practice, and instructor coaching.

10

Comprehensive Crisis Management

Training includes prevention, de-escalation, intervention, post-incident recovery, and documentation strategies.

11

Reduced Risk and Increased Confidence

Organizations report improved staff confidence, safer environments, and better management of challenging situations.

12

Ongoing Support

DACI is more than a class—we provide consultation, guidance, and continued support to help organizations build a culture of safety.
